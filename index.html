How It Works
Every step happens on your device.


1. Paste
Paste your error, code, or project information.


2. Analyze
CodeFix checks the information against its local rule-based knowledge base.


3. Generate
Receive a structured explanation or developer content.


About
CodeFix Generator is a lightweight rule-based developer assistant designed for students, beginner programmers, and developers who want quick explanations and documentation tools without relying on cloud AI services.
No API keys. No external AI. No cloud processing.

Privacy: your input never leaves the browser. History is saved only in your browser's LocalStorage.
